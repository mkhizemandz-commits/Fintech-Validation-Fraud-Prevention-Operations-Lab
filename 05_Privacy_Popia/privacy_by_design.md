# POPIA & Privacy-by-Design

## Purpose

This section demonstrates how privacy principles can be incorporated into a customer-validation and fraud-prevention workflow in South Africa.

The approach is informed by the principles of the **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)** and is intended as a practical portfolio demonstration rather than legal advice.

---

# Why Privacy Matters in Validation

Identity validation may involve personal information such as identity-document information, contact details and information used to assess application risk.

Fraud prevention therefore needs to protect legitimate customers while using personal information responsibly.

The goal should be:

**Use the information necessary to perform the validation task, protect it appropriately, and avoid unnecessary exposure.**

---

# Practical Privacy Principles

## 1. Purpose Limitation

Customer information should be used for legitimate and clearly defined purposes.

For example, information collected for identity validation should not automatically be reused for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal or organisational basis.

---

## 2. Data Minimisation

Only information necessary for the task should be accessed or recorded.

A validation representative should avoid collecting, copying or retaining additional personal information simply because it is available.

### Example

If a case can be resolved using an existing verification result, there may be no need to request additional personal information from the customer.

---

## 3. Access Control

Access to customer information should be limited to authorised employees and systems.

A representative should only access information required for their role and should not access customer records out of curiosity or for unrelated purposes.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

Customer information should not be shared with unauthorised people.

This includes avoiding unnecessary disclosure of:

* Identity information
* Contact information
* Application information
* Fraud indicators
* Information belonging to another customer

Customer identity should also be appropriately verified before discussing sensitive account or application information through a support interaction.

---

## 5. Secure Handling

Sensitive information should be handled using approved company systems and processes.

Employees should avoid storing customer information in personal devices, personal cloud storage, messaging applications or other unauthorised systems.

Information should only be transferred or recorded through approved channels.

---

## 6. Accurate Information

Incorrect information can negatively affect legitimate customers.

Where appropriate, validation processes should allow errors or legitimate discrepancies to be identified and resolved.

Where an important discrepancy is identified, it should be documented accurately and handled according to the applicable process rather than being treated as evidence of fraud automatically.

---

## 7. Retention

Personal information should not be retained indefinitely without a legitimate reason.

Information should be handled according to the organisation's approved retention requirements and applicable legal obligations.

A representative should not independently create personal copies of customer information for future reference.

---

# Privacy in Fraud Investigations

Fraud prevention creates an important balance.

A representative needs enough information to investigate suspicious activity, but should avoid unnecessary exposure of personal data.

### Example

If multiple applications are linked to the same phone number, the representative should investigate the relationship between the applications without unnecessarily exposing the identities or personal information of unrelated customers.

The investigation should focus on the information necessary to resolve the case.

---

# Privacy and Customer Communication

When communicating with customers, I would avoid revealing sensitive internal fraud controls.

For example, instead of explaining:

> "Your application was flagged because our system gives 20 points for this particular device pattern."

I would provide a general explanation that additional verification is required and explain the customer's next available step.

This protects the integrity of fraud controls while maintaining clear and useful communication.

---

# Privacy and False Positives

Privacy and fraud prevention are connected to customer fairness.

An incorrect fraud decision can create unnecessary friction for a legitimate customer.

For this reason, risk indicators should support investigation rather than automatically being treated as proof of fraudulent behaviour.

Where a legitimate explanation exists, it should be considered alongside the other available evidence.

---

# Practical Validation Checklist

Before accessing, recording or sharing customer information, I would consider:

### Is the information necessary?

If not, avoid collecting or accessing it.

### Am I authorised to access it?

If not, do not attempt to access it. Follow the appropriate escalation process.

### Is it being handled securely?

Use approved company systems and procedures.

### Am I authorised to disclose it?

Verify the customer appropriately before discussing sensitive information and avoid disclosing information belonging to another person.

### Am I exposing another customer's information?

If yes, stop and follow the appropriate privacy process.

### Is the information accurate?

If there is an apparent discrepancy, investigate rather than making assumptions.

### Does the customer understand what they need to do?

Provide clear and appropriate communication without revealing confidential security controls.

### Have I documented the interaction appropriately?

Record relevant information accurately using approved systems without creating unnecessary copies of personal information.

---

# Privacy-by-Design Principle

My approach can be summarised as:

**Validate carefully. Investigate responsibly. Use only necessary information. Protect customer privacy.**
