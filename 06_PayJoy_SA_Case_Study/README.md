# PayJoy South Africa — Validation Improvement Case Study

## Overview

This section applies the validation, fraud-prevention, customer-support and privacy concepts developed throughout this portfolio to a fictional fintech operating environment.

The case study uses publicly available information about PayJoy together with synthetic data and an independent methodology.

It does **not** claim access to PayJoy's internal systems, fraud rules, risk models, thresholds, customer data or operating procedures.

---

## Case Study Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how I would approach a validation-support environment by considering:

* Identity and document validation
* Potential fraud indicators
* Risk prioritisation
* Manual investigation
* Customer experience
* False-positive risk
* Privacy and confidentiality
* Escalation
* Root-cause analysis
* Operational improvement

---

## Proposed Workflow

The case study follows this general workflow:

**Application → Validation → Risk Indicators → Investigation → Customer Communication → Decision/Escalation → Documentation → Operational Feedback**

The emphasis is on using risk indicators to prioritise investigation rather than automatically treating them as proof of fraud.

---

## Proposed Improvement

The case study proposes a **Validation Review Feedback Dashboard** that could help an operations team monitor:

* Application volumes
* Manual-review volumes
* Risk distribution
* Common validation indicators
* Investigation outcomes
* Escalation volume
* Validation-related customer contacts
* Potential false-positive patterns
* Recurring technical issues
* Review turnaround time

The purpose is to demonstrate how operational data could be used to identify patterns and support continuous improvement.
